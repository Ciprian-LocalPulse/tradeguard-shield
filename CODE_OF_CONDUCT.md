# Code of Conduct

## Academic and professional standard

TradeGuard Shield is an independent research and engineering project. Its
community is expected to protect intellectual honesty, reproducibility,
privacy, and the dignity of people and organisations discussed by the work.

Participants must:

- distinguish observation, inference, hypothesis, and verified fact;
- cite sources and identify uncertainty, missing data, and methodological limits;
- preserve the author’s copyright, NOTICE, and attribution when reusing the work;
- label forks, modified distributions, benchmarks, and derivative research clearly;
- criticise code, evidence, methods, or claims rather than people;
- protect secrets, personal data, credentials, and non-public financial information;
- report security issues privately and responsibly before public disclosure;
- welcome independent replication, correction, and good-faith disagreement.

## Prohibited conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, impersonation, plagiarism,
fabrication of evidence, malicious reporting, credential theft, publication
of private information, and presenting a modified distribution as an official
TradeGuard Shield release are not acceptable.

No contributor may use project outputs to make unsupported accusations of
fraud, criminality, insolvency, or regulatory status. Risk indicators are not
legal findings, financial advice, or proof of wrongdoing.

## Attribution and derivative work

The Apache License 2.0 permits reuse and modification subject to its terms.
Reuse must retain the copyright and NOTICE material, modified files must be
identified, and derivative distributions must not imply endorsement or claim
that their changes were authored by Ciprian Ștefan Pleșca. The project name,
logos, and marks are not granted for misleading association.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove content, reject contributions, restrict repository
access, or decline collaboration when conduct violates this code or threatens
the safety, privacy, attribution, or research integrity of the project.

For sensitive reports, contact [contact@agentflow-enterrprise.com](mailto:contact@agentflow-enterrprise.com).
