# Governance

TradeGuard Shield is authored and maintained by Ciprian Ștefan Pleșca.

## Decision Principles

- User protection
- Evidence quality
- Privacy by design
- Explainability
- Security and maintainability
- Responsible disclosure

## Maintainer Authority

The maintainer controls roadmap, releases, security response, repository settings, and final merge decisions.

Contributions do not grant ownership, governance control, private access, or influence over security decisions.
