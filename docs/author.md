## Research position

The project is a self-directed public-interest investigation into explainable, low-cost safeguards for people encountering online trading platforms. The author’s position is methodological rather than accusatory: a risk indicator should expose evidence and uncertainty, preserve a route for correction, and avoid claiming a legal conclusion.
